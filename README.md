# Project AEGIS-FAILSAFE: Dual-Vector Systemic Risk & Resilient Governance

> **A Federal Risk Architecture Addressing the Velocity Paradox Between Machine-Speed AI Exploitation and Cascading Patch Failures Across National Critical Functions.**

---

## 📌 Executive Overview
Modern cybersecurity operations face an asymmetric structural trap:
* **The Machine-Speed Threat:** Adversaries deploy autonomous AI agents capable of continuous reconnaissance, polymorphic payload iteration, and sub-second lateral traversal—collapsing defensive decision cycles (OODA loops).
* **The Fragile Defensive Reaction:** Defensive teams rush out-of-band updates and monolithic security definitions directly into privileged runtime and kernel spaces.
* **The Velocity Paradox:** By compressing validation cadences to match adversary velocity, organizations bypass canary rings and progressive staging, inadvertently transforming defensive updates into systemic single points of failure (SPOFs) that trigger widespread infrastructure outages.

## 🏛️ Statutory, Regulatory & Governance Baselines

This project synthesizes federal policy directives and systems engineering standards into actionable defense controls:

* **Executive Order 14028 (*Improving the Nation's Cybersecurity*)**: Section 4 Critical Software definitions, mandatory provenance verification, and integrity testing.
* **National Security Memorandum 22 (NSM-22)**: Critical Infrastructure Security and Resilience coordinator mandates under CISA.
* **NIST Cybersecurity Framework (CSF) 2.0**: Enterprise governance (`GV`) and continuous improvement mechanisms.
* **NIST SP 800-53 Rev. 5**: Configuration Management (`CM-3`, `CM-4`), System Integrity (`SI-7`), and System Architecture (`SA-8`).
* **NIST SP 800-161 Rev. 1**: Cybersecurity Supply Chain Risk Management (C-SCRM) and privileged vendor channel validation.
* **NIST SP 800-218 (SSDF v1.1)**: Secure Software Development Framework verification gates and parameter validation.
* **NIST AI Risk Management Framework (AI RMF 1.0)**: Operational bounds, trustworthiness measurement, and circuit breakers for autonomous agentic tooling.
